SAIGallery is a museum of AI-generated images found in the wild. Nothing here was made for this gallery: every exhibit is a genuine image that someone published in a public post on LinkedIn or another social media platform, to an audience of colleagues, customers or followers.

Those posts were meant to be engaging, or to explain, announce or teach something. The images chosen to carry them were supposed to help. Instead, they confuse, distract, or undermine the very message they were attached to, and they were published anyway.

## What you will and won't find here

Each exhibit shows the image itself and a curatorial note about what went wrong with it. The notes critique the image and the choice to publish it unexamined, never the person who posted it.

You won't find where any image came from: no links, no names, no platforms, no dates. That information isn't shown, and it isn't kept. The gallery exists to document a practice, not to point at people.
